The digital divide

Tech fluency is no longer optional — but most schools can't offer it

Fewer than 15% of Nigerian secondary schools currently offer practical, globally aligned tech training. In response, Emoji Digitals, in strategic collaboration with the Cisco Networking Academy, presents the Cisco-Emoji Digitals Tech & Creative Skills Program.

It is a school-based initiative aimed at bridging this digital divide and equipping students with world-class tech and creative skills — without adding a heavy tech burden to your school.
