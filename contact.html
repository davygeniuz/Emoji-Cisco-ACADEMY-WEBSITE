Quick answers

A computer lab with a minimum of 10 PCs (Core i5+, 8GB RAM), internet of at least 10 Mbps broadband, the required software (Python 3.x, Cisco Packet Tracer, Adobe tools), and a designated teacher to act as coordinator. If you're missing any of these, we support setup partnerships with Cisco NETACAD.

Each term runs for 3 months, aligned with the academic calendar, with 1–3 sessions per week based on your plan and a maximum of 30 students per batch.

SS1 to SS3 students, ages 12–18. They can follow the Tech Track (Python, cybersecurity, AI, networking, software & web development, data) or the Creative Track (graphic design, video editing, digital marketing) — or both.

Scheduling of the number of visits for the Basic and Hybrid programs is negotiated according to the number of students — we'll work around your timetable.
