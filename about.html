Our story

Tech fluency is the foundation of every future career

In today's digital world, tech fluency is no longer optional — it is the foundation of every future career. Yet fewer than 15% of Nigerian secondary schools currently offer practical, globally aligned tech training.

In response, Emoji Digitals, in strategic collaboration with the Cisco Networking Academy, presents the Cisco-Emoji Digitals Tech & Creative Skills Program — a revolutionary, school-based initiative aimed at bridging this digital divide and equipping students with world-class tech and creative skills.

Cisco NetAcad is already in partnership with 53 Federal and Command Schools in Nigeria, and we are inviting more schools to join this powerful movement. We formally invite your school to become part of it.
