Emoji Cisco Networking Academy has been turning curious beginners into certified network engineers in Lagos since 2016 — one lab at a time.

Our story

Started in one room with four routers and a whiteboard

In 2016, our founder — then a carrier network engineer — was tired of interviewing graduates who could recite the OSI model but had never logged into a router. So he rented a small room in Lekki, bought second-hand lab equipment, and started teaching the only way he knew worked: by doing.

Ten years later the academy has grown into a full training campus with virtual labs, certified instructors and hiring partners across Nigeria. What hasn't changed is the rule every class still follows: no concept is taught until the student has configured it, broken it, and fixed it again.

We teach total beginners and seasoned engineers side by side — because networking rewards people who understand fundamentals deeply and specialists who keep pushing past them.
