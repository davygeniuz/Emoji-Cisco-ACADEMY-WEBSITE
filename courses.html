How the program runs in your school

Term duration3 months, aligned with the academic calendar
Weekly sessions1–3 per week (based on plan)

School requirements

To join, your school must have:

Computer labMinimum 10 PCs (Core i5+, 8GB RAM)
InternetMinimum 10 Mbps broadband
SoftwarePython 3.x, Cisco Packet Tracer, Adobe tools
CoordinatorA designated teacher to assist with sessions
